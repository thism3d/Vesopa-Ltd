## Description

Metric Membership is the official members' app for car parks and sites run by METRIC Group Ltd, the UK parking and ANPR specialist.

Your car is your pass. Add your registration once, and Metric's number plate recognition cameras know your car. When you drive up to a member barrier, the camera reads your plate, checks your membership and lifts the barrier in a moment. It works the same on the way out. You don't need a ticket, a fob, a card to swipe or an intercom.

Sign in with Continue with Vesopa, the secure sign-in used across Vesopa apps. If you don't have an account yet, it creates one in a minute using a code sent to your email. New memberships are checked and approved by Metric's team, and the app shows your status until yours is active.

Everything about your membership is in one place:

- Your digital membership card, with your member number, plan, status and a QR code for staff to scan.
- Your cars. Register up to the number your plan allows, and add, change or remove them from your desk or your phone. Changes reach the barriers automatically.
- Your visits. See when each car arrived and left, at which site and through which gate.
- Your account. Keep your name, phone number and company up to date, see the sites your membership covers, or delete your account and every car on it.

The app is built and run for Metric Group by Vesopa Software Ltd. Your details are used only to run your membership and open the barriers you're entitled to use.

For help with your membership, call Metric on 01793 647800.

## Features

1. Number plate entry: registered cars open Metric barriers on the way in and out.
2. Digital membership card with member number, plan, status and QR code.
3. Add, change and remove your cars at any time, with changes sent to the barriers automatically.
4. Visit history showing every arrival and departure by site and gate.
5. Secure sign-in with Continue with Vesopa, with no new password to remember.
