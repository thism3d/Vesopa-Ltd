Privacy policy

Metric Membership · last updated 27 September 2026

Who we are

Metric Membership is the members' app for car parks and sites run by METRIC Group Ltd ("Metric", "we"). Metric decides how membership works and is responsible for your information. Vesopa Software Ltd builds and hosts the app and its server at metric.vesopa.com for Metric, and runs Continue with Vesopa, the sign-in the app uses.

What we collect

  • Your details: your name and email address from your Vesopa sign-in, plus any phone number and company you add. We also keep your member number, plan, membership status and dates.
  • Your cars: the registration of each car you add, and any make, colour and nickname you give it.
  • Barrier records: each time a Metric camera reads one of your cars, we record the registration, the site, the gate, the time, whether you were going in or out, and whether the barrier opened. The cameras' pictures are not stored by this app.
  • An activity log: what you press and change in the app (for example "added a car"), the time, the app version and your IP address. We use it to find faults and to settle questions about changes to a membership.

We don't use advertising, tracking or analytics services, and we don't sell your information.

Why we use it

  • To run your membership: to check you're entitled to use a site and open the barrier for your registered cars. This is the service you asked for.
  • To show you your membership card, your cars and your visits.
  • To keep the service secure and working, and to answer questions about a visit or a change. This is in our legitimate interests and yours.

Your registrations are sent to the barrier cameras at the sites your membership covers, so they can recognise your car. Metric's staff can see your details, cars and visits in the membership console.

How long we keep it

If you delete your account in the app (Account, then Delete account), your cars are taken off every barrier straight away. We also remove your name, phone number and company at the same time. We keep your email address and member number as a record that the membership existed and was closed. If you'd like those removed too, ask us using the details below.

On your device

The app keeps your sign-in and your display preferences on your device, so you stay signed in. Signing out removes your sign-in. On the web, the app's engine is loaded from Google's servers (gstatic.com), as with every Flutter web app.

Your rights

You can see and change your details and cars in the app at any time. You can also ask us for a copy of your information, to correct it, to delete it, or to object to how we use it. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
