What's new in Vesopa Go

Every release, newest first. Vesopa Go is in open testing on iPhone, iPad, Android and Android TV.

Get the newest version Direct download for Android and TV

  1. 1.2.73 19 November 2026 Android TV

    • Your phone is the TV's remote. More › Control a screen or TV, on Android, iPhone and iPad: arrows, OK, Back, adverts, reload, and Settings after a manager's PIN on the phone.
    • Only what the device can do. Go checks for a camera, NFC and a touchscreen first, so a TV no longer offers card tapping or pass scanning, and types the number instead.
    • Settings PIN with the remote. The number buttons, or Up, Down, Right and OK, with no on-screen keyboard.
    • Choose the colour with the remote on the first screen.
    • Every Android TV can install it. Camera, Bluetooth and location are no longer required, so Google Play offers Go to every TV.

  2. 1.2.72 19 November 2026 Payments

    • Wallet card readers. The member card sheet also reads a Wallet NFC reader that types the card number, by USB or Bluetooth.
    • Android TV download on go.vesopa.com/install, with step-by-step help.

  3. 1.2.71 19 November 2026 Android TV

    • A proper TV app. On a TV, Go offers just Kitchen and Customer display, and the remote works on every screen.
    • The kitchen grid on a TV. Tickets side by side on the wall; a long ticket says "press OK to see all".

  4. 1.2.70 19 November 2026 Loyalty

    • Tap a member's phone or watch. Go reads a club's member card by NFC from an Android phone or Wear OS watch, as well as plastic cards and fobs.

  5. 1.2.68 and 1.2.69 18 November 2026 Sign-up

    • Sign up online. A guided set-up, one step at a time, in the app or at go.vesopa.com/signup, with only that step's tasks on screen. 14 days free.
    • Scan a TV to connect it. The TV shows a QR code; the phone's camera opens Go at "Connect this screen".
    • Vesopa Tap to Pay on the phone itself, where it is switched on for the venue, and Google Pay or Apple Pay, where available, to pay for the licence.

  6. 1.2.66 18 November 2026 Displays

    • Customer display options to match the Windows display: advert timing, sound and fit, which side the bill is on, idle time, your own thank-you message and QR code, full screen and orientation.
    • The TV remote works on TV boxes that call themselves tablets, and a key press wakes the idle screen.

  7. 1.2.65 17 November 2026 Sign-up

    • The owner's manager PIN is set during sign-up, before the till opens, and the device chooser is animated.
