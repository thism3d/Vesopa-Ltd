Cookie policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

This policy covers go.vesopa.com, Vesopa Go's own website, published by Vesopa Software Ltd (company number 17362206). For our other websites see vesopasoftware.com/cookies.

The short version

go.vesopa.com sets no cookies of its own. We use no analytics, no advertising, no social media pixels and no tracking of any kind, so there is nothing to consent to and no cookie banner.

Kept in your browser, never sent to us

A few small settings are kept in your browser's own storage so the site works the way you left it. They are never sent to our server or anyone else, and clearing your site data removes them.

Other companies' services on our pages

  • The film on the home page is played from YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). YouTube sets no cookies until you press play; once you do, Google's own privacy policy and cookies apply to the film.
  • Sign-up uses Stripe to check your identity and business and to save your card. Stripe sets its own cookies on those steps to prevent fraud, as its cookie policy explains. Stripe is not loaded on any other page.
  • Signing in to your Vesopa account happens on auth.vesopa.com, Vesopa's own sign-in service, which sets a strictly necessary cookie there to keep you signed in. It is not set on go.vesopa.com.

Choosing

Every browser lets you see, block and delete cookies and site data, usually under Settings › Privacy. Blocking everything on go.vesopa.com changes only the remembered colour and sound choice. Blocking Stripe's cookies stops the sign-up's identity and card steps from working.

Changes and questions

If we ever add a cookie that is not strictly necessary, it will stay off until you switch it on, and this page will change first. Questions to info@vesopa.com.
