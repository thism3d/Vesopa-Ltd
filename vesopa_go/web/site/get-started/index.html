Get started with Vesopa Go

For pubs, clubs, restaurants and cafés in the United Kingdom.

Vesopa Go is the mobile companion to Vesopa EPOS. Your staff take orders and contactless payments at the table or the counter, with Tap to Pay on iPhone on an iPhone, or your Dojo card machine on an iPad or Android device. Everything lands in the same back office as your tills.

Card payments are processed by Dojo, Vesopa's accredited payment partner. Your venue is the merchant: the money goes to your own Dojo account.

Apply for your venue

How it works

  1. Apply

    Tell us your venue's name, address and contact, and whether you already take cards with Dojo. We reply within one working day.

    Same day

  2. Dojo verifies your business

    If you already have a Dojo account, there is nothing to do. If not, Dojo opens a merchant account for your venue and carries out its business and identity checks (KYC): your company or sole-trader details, the owner's ID and the bank account for payouts. Dojo also switches on Tap to Pay on iPhone for your account.

    Usually 1–3 working days for a new Dojo account

  3. We set up your venue

    We add Vesopa Go to your Vesopa back office, connect it to your Dojo account and load your menu, staff and tables. You choose who may take payments and give refunds.

    Same day once Dojo has approved you

  4. Download and sign in

    Install Vesopa Go from the App Store on each iPhone and sign in with your venue's Vesopa account. Staff sign on with their own PIN.

  5. Accept the Tap to Pay terms, once

    The first time Tap to Pay on iPhone is used at your venue, the account owner or a manager reads and accepts Apple's terms and conditions in the app. Vesopa Go then shows your staff how to take a contactless payment, and the guide is always available again from Settings.

  6. Take payments

    At checkout choose Tap to Pay on iPhone and the customer holds their contactless card, iPhone, Apple Watch or other digital wallet near the top of the iPhone. Receipts print or go by email.

Minimum time from applying to taking payments: about 1 working day for an existing Dojo customer; 2–4 working days with a new Dojo account.

What you need

  • A venue in the United Kingdom.
  • An iPhone XS or later on the latest version of iOS, for Tap to Pay on iPhone.
  • A Dojo account (or one opened for you in step 2).
  • A Vesopa EPOS back office, which we set up.
