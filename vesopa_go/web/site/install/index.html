Vesopa Go: test builds

Most people should join the test from the home page (TestFlight on iPhone and iPad, Google Play on Android). These files are for installing straight from this site.

iPhoneAndroid phone or tabletAndroid TV

iPhone

Version 1.2.61 (1)

For Vesopa's registered test iPhones only. It carries Tap to Pay on iPhone for testing before Apple approves the app for the App Store.

Install on this iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari on the iPhone and tap Install on this iPhone, then Install.
  2. It replaces the TestFlight copy of Vesopa Go. Sign in again afterwards.
  3. If it says the app cannot be opened: Settings > Privacy & Security > Developer Mode, turn it on and restart the iPhone.

Android

Version 1.2.63 (65)

The same app as Google Play's test track, as a file to install straight from this page.

Download for Android

  1. If Vesopa Go from Google Play is on the phone, uninstall it first: the two are signed differently and Android will not put one over the other. Sign in again afterwards.
  2. Open this page in Chrome on the phone, tap Download for Android, then open the file.
  3. If Android asks, allow Chrome to install unknown apps, then tap Install.

Android TV

Version 1.2.63 (65)

The same app for a TV or a Google TV stick, where it runs as the kitchen screen or the customer display, controlled with the TV remote.

Download for Android TV

  1. On the TV, open Google Play and search for Vesopa Go, if the TV's account is on the test. Otherwise:
  2. Install the free Downloader app from Google Play on the TV, open it and type go.vesopa.com/install/VesopaGo.apk.
  3. When the TV asks, allow Downloader to install unknown apps, then choose Install.
  4. Open Vesopa Go from the TV's apps and choose Kitchen or Customer display.
  5. Connect it: scan the QR code the TV shows with a phone signed in to Vesopa Go. From then on the phone can drive the TV too (More › Control a screen or TV).
