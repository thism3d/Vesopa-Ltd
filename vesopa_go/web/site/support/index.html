Vesopa Go support

We're here every day. Email support@vesopasoftware.com.

Signing a device in

Open Vesopa Go, tap Continue with Vesopa and sign in with your venue's Vesopa account. If you run more than one venue, choose which one. Your staff then sign on with their own PIN.

If it says the venue has no Vesopa Go licences, Vesopa Go hasn't been added to your venue yet: sign up (14 days free), or email us and we'll add it.

Taking a card

With Vesopa Tap to Pay, where it is switched on for your venue, the customer holds their card or phone near the top of the iPhone or the back of the Android phone. Otherwise the payment goes to the venue's card machine (Dojo, Stripe Terminal or Teya): choose which machine under Card machine in the menu. With QR Pay the customer scans the bill and pays on their own phone.

A TV as the kitchen screen or customer display

Install Vesopa Go on an Android TV or Google TV (how), open it and choose Kitchen or Customer display. The TV shows a QR code: scan it with a phone signed in to Vesopa Go to connect it. The TV remote works everywhere, and from a phone, More › Control a screen or TV drives it too. Settings on the TV ask for a manager's PIN, typed with the remote's number buttons.

Receipts

Find your receipt printer under Settings > Receipt printer > Scan, or use AirPrint. Customers can also have their receipt by email.

Settings

Everything about Vesopa Go for your venue is set in your Vesopa back office, under Vesopa Go: card payments, tips, sign-on, sounds, memberships, tickets and who may do what.
