Inside the app

Every step of the payment, on screen.

The same five steps as the Windows till: starting, present card, PIN, processing, complete. The customer sees where to hold their card, and the clerk sees exactly where the payment has got to.

Step 1 of 5

  1. 01

    Take the order

    The till's own buttons and colours, on the phone. Tap, add a note, choose the options the kitchen needs.

  2. 02

    Check the bill

    Seats, deals and discounts, exactly as the till works them out. Split it, print it, or send it to the till.

  3. 03

    Choose how to pay

    Cash, Tap to Pay, the venue's card machine, QR Pay, a gift card or a voucher: the till's own pay screen.

  4. 04

    Or let them scan

    QR Pay puts the bill on the customer's own phone, with every item listed, and closes it the moment it's paid.

  5. 05

    Done, and in the books

    Approved in seconds. The sale is in the back office at once, with the receipt printed, emailed or shown.

Payments

Three ways to take the money, none of them a queue.

  • Vesopa Tap to PayThe customer holds their contactless card, phone or watch to your phone: Tap to Pay on iPhone, and on Android phones, where it is switched on for your venue. No extra hardware.
  • QR PayA code on the screen or the printed bill. The customer scans it and pays on their own phone by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay; the bill closes itself.
  • The venue's card machineSend the amount to the venue's Dojo, Stripe Terminal or Teya machine from any device, with tips and split bills. Or make a spare phone the card terminal for your tills.

The venue network

When the Wi-Fi fails, the till becomes the Wi-Fi.

Go, the tills, the kitchen screens and the customer displays talk over the venue's own network, encrypted, so tables move in milliseconds. If the venue Wi-Fi drops or puts phones on a separate network, the Windows till can share its own network as a hotspot from Settings › Network and hardware, and the phones join it.

The same page shows every adapter, Bluetooth and USB device on the till, with warnings and restarts, and Go shows its own connection, the response time and the tills it can see.

Questions

Asked and answered.

Do I need Vesopa EPOS tills?

Every Vesopa Go venue has a Vesopa back office: signing up makes yours, with your menu, staff and tables. Go works on its own, or beside Vesopa EPOS tills on Windows, sharing open bills with them as they happen.

How much does it cost?

14 days free, then £29 a month for the Vesopa Go licence. Add a card when you sign up; nothing is taken until the free days end. Card payment fees are your payment provider's.

How do I take card payments?

Three ways: Vesopa Tap to Pay on the phone itself where it is switched on for your venue, the venue's Dojo, Stripe Terminal or Teya card machine, or QR Pay, where the customer scans the bill and pays on their own phone.

What happens if the internet drops?

Orders wait safely on the device and go to the back office, in order, when the connection is back. If the venue Wi-Fi fails, the Windows till can share its own network as a hotspot and the phones join it.

Can I use a TV as the kitchen screen?

Yes. Install Vesopa Go on an Android TV or Google TV, choose Kitchen or Customer display, and drive it with the TV remote or from your phone. How to install it on a TV.

Is card data safe?

Cards are read by the payment provider's certified software and go straight to them. Vesopa Go never sees or stores a card number. Payments and card security.

Where do I download it?

Vesopa Go is in open testing: on iPhone and iPad through TestFlight, and on Android through Google Play's test. Android phones and TVs can also install it straight from this site.

How do I get help?

Email support@vesopasoftware.com any day, or see support.
