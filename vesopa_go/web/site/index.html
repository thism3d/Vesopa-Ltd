Your till, on every iPad and phone in the venue.

Vesopa Go is Vesopa EPOS away from the counter. Seat a table, send the order to the kitchen and take the payment where the customer is, with every sale in the same back office as your tills.

Add Vesopa Go to your venue Support
1

The till's own screens

The buttons, colours and pages you programmed for the till, on iPad and phone. Managers can arrange them for each kind of device.

2

Tables and the kitchen

The floor plan as you drew it. Seat a table, add covers, send courses to the kitchen, hold the mains and fire them when it's time.

3

Card payments with Dojo

Tap to Pay on iPhone, a Dojo Pocket, or the venue's Dojo card machine. Tips, split bills and receipts by print or email.

4

Customers and loyalty

Find a customer, add their loyalty points and discount to the bill, sell memberships and event tickets, check tickets in at the gate.

5

The same rules as the till

Staff sign on with their PIN, card or face. Voids, refunds, discounts and the drawer follow each person's permissions, with a manager's PIN when needed.

6

One back office

Open bills shared with the tills in an instant, over the venue's own network. Every setting lives in the Vesopa back office.

For venues that use Vesopa EPOS

Vesopa Go is added to a venue's licences by Vesopa. Your manager signs each iPad or phone in once with your Vesopa account, and your staff sign on with their own PIN.

To add it, email info@vesopasoftware.com.
