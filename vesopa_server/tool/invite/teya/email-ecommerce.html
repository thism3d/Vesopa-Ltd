What we have built with Teya

1
 On the till: Vesopa EPOS takes card payments through Teya's SDK on Windows (POSLink on Android), chosen per venue from our back office beside Dojo and Barclaycard.
2
 Online: our online menu takes payment on Teya's hosted checkout. The Vesopa Kitchen, our demo venue, runs it on the Teya sandbox: menu.vesopa.com/vesopakitchen.
3
 Results: the outcome is read from Teya after the redirect and from Teya's webhook, so an order is paid exactly once, and a cancelled order is refunded through the API.

Trying the demo

1
 Open menu.vesopa.com/vesopakitchen and add something to the basket.
2
 Choose to pay by card. You go to Teya's checkout; pay with a Teya sandbox test card.
3
 You return to the order page, which shows the payment result; the order goes to the venue's till and kitchen screen only once it is paid.
