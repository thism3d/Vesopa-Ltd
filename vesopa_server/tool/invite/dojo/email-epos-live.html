What we have built on the sandbox

1
 Sign-up and trial: a venue picks a plan on vesopaepos.com/trial. A £1 payment on Dojo's hosted checkout confirms the card, Apple Pay or Google Pay, and is refunded through the API straight away. A 14-day free trial then starts.
2
 First charge and renewals: a payment intent per invoice, paid on Dojo's hosted checkout (pay.dojo.tech) from an emailed link, by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. Card details are only ever entered on Dojo's page.
3
 Failed or unpaid payments: retried on day 2, 4 and 7, with an email each time and a fresh payment link. After that the subscription is suspended, and the venue's tills pause after a 48-hour grace period. They come back on as soon as the payment is captured.
4
 Status: we only treat Captured as paid. The webhook names the payment intent, and we always re-read its status from the API before acting. Every POST carries an idempotency key.
5
 Refunds: full or partial, through the API, from our admin console. Each one is recorded with who made it.
6
 Sandbox checks done: payment intents created and read back, a setup intent created (Unscheduled), and a refund before capture refused as expected.
