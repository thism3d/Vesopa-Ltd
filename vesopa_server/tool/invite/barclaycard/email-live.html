Hello Barclaycard team,

I am the developer at Vesopa Software Ltd, which makes the Vesopa EPOS, back office and online ordering used by hospitality venues and clubs in the UK. A client asked us to offer Barclaycard, so we have built Smartpay Fuse into Vesopa on our sandbox account (Organization ID vesopa_uk_1792070796), and we would now like to take it live.

What is built

✓  Online menu: Barclaycard's Unified Checkout
Guests order at their table and pay on Barclaycard's secure card form, which opens straight away. Paid orders reach the till and kitchen already paid. Live demo on our sandbox: menu.vesopa.com/vesopakitchen.
✓  EPOS till: pay on the customer's phone
Card on the till shows a QR code (on the till and the customer display). The customer pays by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay on Barclaycard's form, and the sale closes on the till by itself. Cancel, status check and refunds to the card from the till.
✓  Back office: per venue, sandbox and live
Each venue saves its own Smartpay Fuse keys (checked with Barclaycard before saving, shared secret stored encrypted) and chooses Dojo, Teya or Barclaycard for its tills and its online menu in one tap.
✓  Tested on the sandbox
Payments, refunds, voids and capture contexts (HTTP Signature, REST) against api.smartpayfuse-test.barclaycard, on our test venue The Vesopa Kitchen.
