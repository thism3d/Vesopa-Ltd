You can sign in with an email address and password, an emailed one-time code, an SMS one-time code, or by linking Google, Apple, Microsoft or GitHub. You can add multi-factor authentication using an authenticator app, SMS, or a passkey.

If you tick "remember this device", we keep you signed in on that device until you sign out or revoke it. Only do that on a device other people cannot use. You can see and revoke every remembered device from your account.
