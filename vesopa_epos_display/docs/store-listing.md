## Product description

Vesopa Customer Display is the screen that faces your customer while their order is being rung up on Vesopa EPOS.

It runs on the same PC as your till, on a second monitor. While a sale is in progress the screen is split: their bill on one side, your adverts on the other. When the till is idle, or a bill has been left untouched for as long as you set, the adverts take the whole screen. The moment anything is rung up, the bill comes straight back.

FINDS YOUR TILL BY ITSELF

Install it on the same PC as Vesopa EPOS and there is nothing to type in and no address to configure. It locates the till on its own, and keeps looking until it does — so it makes no difference whether the display or the till is switched on first in the morning.

SET UP FROM THE TILL, NOT FROM THE SCREEN

Everything this display does is on one page in Vesopa EPOS, under Settings then Customer display. Which monitor it uses. Whether it fills that screen. Where your adverts come from and how long each one stays up. How the screen is split. What the customer reads. You change it standing at the till and look up to see it happen, so nobody has to walk round the counter to a screen with no keyboard in front of it. That page also tells you whether a display is connected at all.

YOUR ADVERTS ARE JUST A FOLDER

Point it at a folder on the PC and drop pictures or clips into it. They appear on the screen without restarting anything, and nothing is uploaded anywhere. PNG, JPG, GIF, WEBP, BMP, MP4, M4V and MOV all play, in file-name order, so naming them 01, 02, 03 sets the order.

Video plays through a decoder built into the app rather than whatever happens to be installed on the PC, so a promo cut by an agency plays on the machine under your counter. Clips are silent unless you turn the sound up: a screen on a counter playing a soundtrack at somebody waiting to be served is usually a complaint rather than a feature.

LAID OUT FOR YOUR COUNTER

Put the bill on whichever side your customer actually stands, and set how much of the screen it takes — a narrow bill if your adverts are upright posters, a wide one if you ring up long rounds. Adverts can sit inside their panel or be cropped to fill it. You can add a standing line across the bottom for the thing your staff would otherwise have to say to everybody: ask about our loyalty card, kitchen closes at nine.

The type is large on a dark screen on purpose. Every figure on it is read from three or four feet away, across a counter, by somebody who is not wearing their glasses. When a sale is paid for, the screen shows what was tendered and the change due, so a customer can check it themselves.

IT CANNOT SLOW YOUR TILL DOWN

This is a separate application, deliberately. Its only connection to the till is one small file, so a display doing video work cannot affect a sale, and a display that stops takes nothing with it: the till carries on selling and the only thing lost is the picture facing the customer.

NOTHING LEAVES THE MACHINE

The app declares no network capability at all. Your bills and your adverts stay on the PC they are on.

WHAT YOU NEED

Windows 10 version 1809 or later, 64-bit, a second monitor, and Vesopa EPOS installed on the same PC. Without the till there is nothing for this display to follow.

## What's new in this version

Version 1.6.2.0 – Pairing, and the Customer's Card on the Screen

Connecting to the till is now four digits. This screen shows a code, the till puts the request in front of whoever is standing at it, and Connect is the whole setup — no paths, nothing to type in, nothing to browse for. The pairing survives an upgrade or a reinstall of either app, so nobody sets it up a second time.

It used to work the till's data folder out for itself, and on a PC that had run more than one till it could attach to a folder nobody writes to and show adverts for ever, with no way to tell that from working normally. That guesswork is gone.

When it is not connected it now says why, in the order worth checking: whether the till is installed on this PC, whether it is running, and whether somebody has signed in. Each answer offers the one thing that helps.

The till can also put a customer's own loyalty or gift card on this screen as a code they scan into their phone, so they add it standing where they are rather than leaning across the counter. Whatever was on screen before comes back afterwards.

Requires Vesopa EPOS 1.6.2.0 or later on the same PC.
