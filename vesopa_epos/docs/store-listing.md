## What's new in this version

Version 1.6.4.0 - Order From The Table

QR Menus And Dine-In: Print a code for every table. A customer scans it, reads your menu on their own phone and sends an order straight to the till - no app to install and no account to make. ## Product description

Vesopa EPOS is a till for pubs, restaurants, cafes and shops, built for the counter rather than adapted to it.

TAKING MONEY

Card and cash on the same screen, with card payments taken through an integrated terminal so nobody is typing a total into a second device and hoping it matches. Split a bill, part-pay it, take a deposit, or put it on a tab. Refunds are handled properly rather than as a negative sale.

TABLES, TABS AND ROUNDS

Open a bill against a table or a name, add to it across a shift, and pick it up from any till in the building. Covers, notes, and modifiers that sit under the item they belong to, so a round of gins with different mixers reads the way the customer ordered it.

THE KITCHEN

Send each part of an order where it belongs: starters to one printer, the grill to another, drinks to the bar. Kitchen tickets can go to printers, to kitchen screens, or both, and each product decides its own route. USB printers are driven directly, without going through the Windows spooler.

WHEN THE BROADBAND GOES

The till keeps selling. Everything is held locally and synced back the moment the connection returns, because a pub does not stop serving because a router rebooted.

THE END OF THE NIGHT

A Z report that closes the period and starts the next one clean: takings by tender, voids counted and valued, no-sales, covers, average spend, and the float carried forward. Reports can also be scheduled to send themselves by email, so the figures are in an inbox before anybody asks for them.

SETTING IT UP

Programme your own keys — what they say, what colour they are, where they sit — so the screen matches the way your bar is laid out rather than the way a spreadsheet was exported. Import a whole catalogue from a workbook to get started, and manage prices, staff and permissions from the back office in a browser.

THE CUSTOMER'S SIDE OF THE COUNTER

Vesopa Customer Display is a companion app for a second screen facing the customer: their bill as it is rung up, your own adverts the rest of the time, and what they paid and the change due at the end. It installs on this same PC, connects to this till with a four-digit code, and is configured entirely from this till's settings.

WHAT YOU NEED

Windows 10 version 1809 or later, 64-bit. A touch screen is ideal but not required. Card payments require a supported payment terminal and a merchant agreement with the payment provider.

Sold and supported by Vesopa EPOS Limited.
