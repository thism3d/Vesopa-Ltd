Version 1.0.9.0 - Memberships and classes

If your venue runs memberships, your card now shows your plan, whether it is active, frozen or waiting for approval, the date it runs to, and everybody on your family plan.

Join a plan or renew your membership from the app and pay by card. Your new date shows as soon as the payment goes through.

Book classes from the app: see the next two weeks, how many places are left, and book, cancel or join the waiting list.
