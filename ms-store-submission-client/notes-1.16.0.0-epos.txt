Version 1.16.0.0 - Card payments with Dojo

Clear results: after every card payment the till shows Approved, Declined, Cancelled or Not confirmed, and keeps it on screen for 5, 10 or 15 seconds (your choice in Settings > Card payments). Press Done to close it sooner.

Cancel and check: cancel a payment from the till, or check with Dojo whether it went through. If the machine stops answering, check the payment or try again.

Refunds: refund a card payment in full or in part from Functions > Card Machine, from a receipt, or from Receipts. A manager can also make a refund without a sale.

Safer setup: the till checks the API key and the card machine before it saves, and your venue's Dojo settings can be set once in the back office.

Card log: every card payment, including failed ones, is kept with Dojo's receipt and sent to the back office.

Customer display: shows each step of the card payment and how it ended.
