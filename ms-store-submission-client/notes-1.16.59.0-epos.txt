Version 1.16.59.0 - Keyboard everywhere, gift card receipts, tables by room

Every text box opens the till's keyboard again, including Settings and custom text. Number boxes get the number pad.
Topping up a gift card or paying with one always prints a receipt showing what the card now holds.
In a venue with rooms, tables are called by their room (Bar 1, Lounge 1) on the till and the receipt. Table names can repeat in different rooms.
Two tills now keep their tables in step: an older copy from the cloud no longer replaces a newer one from the other till.
New in Settings > Local network: Independent, for a till that shares nothing with the others.
