Version 1.16.56.0 - A bigger keyboard, like an iPad's

Every box on the till now uses one bigger keyboard along the bottom of the screen, laid out like an iPad's UK keyboard, with .?123 and #+= for numbers and symbols. Pull a key down to type the small character above it.
The box being typed into moves up above the keyboard instead of hiding under it.
The bar above the keys has undo, redo, cut, copy, paste and select all, and A- and A+ for the key size.
The keyboard button chooses Dock, Undock, Split (two halves for two thumbs) or Floating (small, anywhere on the screen).
Typing on a real keyboard puts the screen keyboard away and leaves a keyboard button in the corner to bring it back. Drag that button to the other corner if you prefer it there.
Prices, quantities and covers get a large number pad in the same place.
