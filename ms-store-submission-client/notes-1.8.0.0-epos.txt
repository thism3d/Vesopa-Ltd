Version 1.8.0.0 - Stock control, and 33 more reports

Two new keys under Functions. Paid Out records money taken out of the drawer that is not a refund - the window cleaner, a taxi - with who it went to and why, then opens the drawer; it shows on the Z report and the cash expected in the drawer comes down by it. Wastage records something dropped, spilled or sent back: find the product, say how many and why, and the back office takes it off the stock and costs it. Both follow the Paid Out and Wastage staff permissions.

Refunds, no sales and paid outs now reach the back office, so there are Refunds, Expenses and Cashback reports there for the first time, and cashback given on a card payment is recorded with the payment.

The back office gains a Stock Control section: stock levels with value at cost, suppliers, pack sizes such as a case of 24 or an 11 gallon keg, purchase orders with a suggested order from your minimums, deliveries booked in against them, wastage, adjustments, stock takes with a printable count sheet, and spot checks. Every change is kept in a stock ledger, and seven stock reports read it.

The Reports page now offers 38 reports in four groups with a search box - department and sub department sales, payment types, tax, sales by time, clerk and table, covers, profit and GP summaries, weekly grids, sales comparison, clerk attendance and wage percentage among them - and any of them can be scheduled by email.
