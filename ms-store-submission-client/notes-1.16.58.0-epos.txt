Version 1.16.58.0 - Split bills on tables

When one person at a table pays their share, the items they paid for now leave the table as a sale of their own, so the table shows only what is still owed, on every till.
After a share is paid, "Leave on table" saves the table and clears the till: no need to press Save Table.
Tables part-paid on the last version are tidied the next time they are paid on.
