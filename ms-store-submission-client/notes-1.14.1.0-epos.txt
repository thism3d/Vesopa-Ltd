Version 1.14.1.0 - Open price items, quick sales, a sync fix and a tidier sale screen

Open price items: a product can now ask for its price when it is rung up, for things like "Open Food". Turn on "Ask the price at the till" on the product, in the back office or on the till. Staff type the price and a short note saying what it was for, and the note stays on the bill and the receipt.

Pay with nothing on the bill: venues that turn this on in the back office (Settings, Tender) can open the payment screen with an empty bill. An amount keyed there is rung up as a Quick sale, with a note of what it was for, and then taken in the usual way.

Fixed: tills could show "Syncing" with a number that never went down. Every void sent an extra copy that the server refused, so it stayed in the queue. Voids are now sent once.

Fixed: scanning a member's QR code from Apple Wallet, Google Wallet or the Loyalty app now brings up the member, even when they have no card number yet. Before, those codes did nothing when scanned.

Tidier sale screen: the "Back to ..." strip above the products no longer appears on pages that already have a key back to the home page, so the products stay in the same place when you change page.
