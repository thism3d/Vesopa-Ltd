Version 1.15.0.0 - Printing that finishes every slip, print modes, and updates from Vesopa

Fixed: on some printers a receipt, kitchen ticket or Z report printed only its first lines and was not cut, and the Z report looked blank. The till let go of the printer before it had finished reading. On a network printer it now waits for the printer to take everything. On a COM port it checks every byte is written.

Print modes: every printer starts on Standard, which follows your venue's Paper and cutting choice in the back office (Receipt Designer). A printer that needs something different can be set to Custom under Settings, Printers: full, partial or no cut, how much paper to feed before the cut, the other cut command, sending slowly for printers that drop data, a beep for kitchen tickets and the cash drawer socket.

Test print: the test slip now shows the print mode and ends with "END OF TEST". If you can read that line and the paper was cut, the printer is set up correctly.

Fixed: the cash drawer now opens on the printer chosen for it in Settings, Printers, and printing a receipt again uses that printer's character set.

Products: a Sub Department filter beside Department.

Back to home strip: a Screen Programming setting in the back office decides when the "Back to ..." strip shows: only where needed, always or never.

Updates: when Vesopa sets a newer version for your venue, the till offers it between sales. A till from the Microsoft Store is taken to the Store page to update.
