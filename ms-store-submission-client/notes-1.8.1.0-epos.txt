Version 1.8.1.0 - Points earned however a member was attached

Loyalty points are now earned, and a visit counted, whenever a member is on the bill - attached on the sale screen, by swiping their card, or on the payment screen. Before, only a member attached on the payment screen earned; anyone attached earlier reached the end of the sale with nothing recorded, so their Activity page in the loyalty app stayed empty and their visits stayed at nought.

Nothing else changes in this release. Stock control, the thirty-eight reports, Paid Out and Wastage from 1.8.0.0 are as they were.
