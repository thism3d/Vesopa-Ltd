Version 1.16.55.0 - Bill arrows, line discounts and terminal numbers

A picked item on the bill is now just highlighted, with no item box. Void and Cancel take items off.
Arrows beside the totals move down and up the bill, and Select all picks every item.
Functions has a Discount key: money off the highlighted items, or off the whole bill when none are highlighted.
After signing in, the till asks which terminal it is. A replacement till takes over the terminal it replaces, so its sales, X and Z carry on under it.
X and Z reports show the terminal number and name.
