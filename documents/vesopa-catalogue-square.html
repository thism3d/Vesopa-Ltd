What we do

Everything a venue needs to sell, serve and stay in touch.

Take one service or the full set. Every part works with the Vesopa Back Office.

Point of sale

Vesopa EPOS

One till for the counter, the tables, the kitchen and the back office. It keeps selling when the broadband drops, then syncs everything once it is back.

Payments

Card payments with Dojo

The till sends the amount straight to the Dojo card machine, so nobody keys it twice. Take cash, card, contactless, Apple Pay and Google Pay, with Teya and Barclaycard also supported.

Screens

Kitchen, customer display and kiosk

Vesopa Kitchen replaces the kitchen printer with a screen at each station. A customer display shows the bill line by line, and a self-service kiosk lets guests order and pay by card.

Loyalty

Rewards and wallet cards

Members collect points on every visit and spend them against the exact price at the till. Their card sits in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet, ready to scan.

Apps

Custom app development

We design and build apps for iPhone, iPad, Android, Apple Watch and Wear OS under your own name. We also handle the App Store and Google Play listings and testing.

Web

Websites, domains and hosting

Register a domain, host your website and set up email from one control panel. We build business and club websites, and keep them running.

Also from Vesopa

Vesopa Go

Take orders at the table and card payments on the phone itself, with Dojo Tap to Pay. Android and iPhone.

Hardware

Tills, receipt printers, kitchen screens and kiosks, delivered from UK stock. See vesopaepos.com/hardware.

Training and support

We train your staff when you start, and updates and support come with every plan.

In detail

Built for the busiest hour of the week.

Our three core services, and a club that runs on them.

Vesopa EPOS

One till, every part of the floor: the counter, the tables, the kitchen and the office.

  • Keeps trading offline. The menu, prices and open bills live on the till, and it syncs when the connection returns, with nothing typed twice.
  • Table plan for seating, splitting bills and moving guests.
  • Change prices and menus, and read live reports, in the Back Office.
  • A large touch keyboard and light or dark mode for every shift.

Payments with Dojo

Card payments joined to the till, so the bill and the card machine always agree.

  • The till sends the amount to the Dojo terminal. No re-keying and no mistyped totals.
  • Cash, card, contactless, Apple Pay and Google Pay on one bill.
  • Vesopa Go takes Dojo Tap to Pay on a phone at the table.
  • Teya and Barclaycard card machines are supported too.

Club and venue apps

Your own app in the stores, with your name, crest and colours, run from the same Back Office.

  • iPhone, iPad, Android, Apple Watch and Wear OS.
  • Members' cards in Apple Wallet and Google Wallet, with points and a QR code.
  • News, members' sign-in and branding managed in the Back Office.
  • We publish to the App Store and Google Play and run the test releases.

Case study

Pontardawe RFC TBC: club approval

A rugby club with members, a bar and a busy match day. The club wanted its own app, under its own name, that members would actually open.

What we built

The club's members' app, "Pontardawe RFC", for iPhone, Android and the watch, in the club's own red and crest.

How members use it

Members sign in, carry their card in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet, and follow club news. The same card works on the web at member.pontardawerfc.com.

Where it is now

Live on the App Store and Google Play. We also look after the club's website, pontardawerfc.com, and its news.
